# Power Up Placement — Listening scripts (American accent)

Recorded with ElevenLabs (model `eleven_v3`, text-to-dialogue) using the Monglish brand voices.
All answer keys in `data/questions.js` are unchanged — these scripts reproduce the original
placement audio with an American accent instead of British.

| Voice | Used for |
|---|---|
| Monglish Narrator | task instruction + item numbers |
| Mongiz / Imy | child speakers |
| Mother / Father | adult speakers |
| Grandma / Grandpa / Mariam | additional speakers (matching tasks) |

To re-record: edit `_audio_build/script.json`, run `_audio_build/gen.ps1 -Tier N`,
then `bash _audio_build/assemble.sh N`. (Those files are gitignored — they hold the API key.)

## Exercise for Power Up 6  (`audio/listen-6.mp3`)
*Pause after each item: 3.5s*

- **NARR:** Listening. Listen, and match each speaker to a picture.

- **NARR:** One.
- **GRANDPA:** I don't particularly like my job, but I'm not going to quit, because I'm going to retire next year. I work full time at the airport, and I often work at weekends too. - **NARR:** Two.
- **MONGIZ:** My mom's away from home quite a lot. She goes all over the world, and she always brings me interesting presents from faraway places. She works on ships. In fact, she's the boss. She tells all the other sailors what to do. I visited her on a ship, and all the other people on the ship call her Captain. It's very strange.

- **MARIAM:** This is Jamal. He works part time in a computer repair shop, but he's starting a new job next month, working for a big computer games company in California. - **NARR:** Four.
- **FATHER:** My career is very important to me. I live for my work, really. I love it. I don't have a boss, and I can come and go as I please. I'm an architect, and I work on some very interesting projects. For example, at the moment I'm designing a new monkey house for the city zoo.

- **NARR:** Five.

- **NARR:** Six.
